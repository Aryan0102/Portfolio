Subscription

To cancel or change your subscription, open Settings → Manage subscription in Orbit, or iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If you subscribed on another device, tap Restore purchases on the subscribe screen.

Disconnecting a brokerage

In Orbit, open Settings and swipe left on the brokerage under Connections.

Deleting your account

In Orbit, open Settings → Delete account. This deletes everything Orbit stores about you. It doesn't cancel your subscription, which is managed with Apple.
