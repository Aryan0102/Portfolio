Orbit Privacy Policy

Effective: November 27, 2026

Orbit shows your brokerage accounts as one ring: what you own, and how it is split. This policy explains what Orbit collects, why, where it goes and how to delete it. Orbit is run by Aryan Gupta ("we"). Questions: aryansumit0102@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Orbit is read-only. It can't trade, move money or change anything in your brokerage accounts.
  • We never see your brokerage password. You sign in to your broker through Plaid, and the password goes to Plaid and your broker, never to us.
  • We keep only what the app needs: your holdings, a daily record of them, the target allocations you set, your subscription's status, and what it takes to keep you signed in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and everything we store from inside the app, at any time.

What we collect

When you sign in with Apple

  • A user identifier Apple gives us for Orbit. It is unique to Orbit and doesn't reveal your Apple Account.
  • We don't ask Apple for your name or email address, and we don't store them.
  • When you sign in, the app gets a session. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, with when it was created and when it expires (30 days).

When you link a brokerage account (through Plaid)

  • An access key from Plaid that lets Orbit read your holdings. We store it encrypted, and it never leaves our server.
  • Your institution's name (for example, "Robinhood") and Plaid's identifier for the connection.
  • Your investment accounts and holdings as Plaid reports them: account names and the last four digits of the account numbers, the securities you hold, quantities, prices, values and cost basis, and cash balances.

When you subscribe (through Apple)

  • Apple processes the payment. We never see your card, your Apple Account or your billing details.
  • We store the identifiers Apple gives the purchase (the original transaction identifier, and the identifier of the subscription product), the subscription's status (for example free trial, active, expired or refunded), when the current period ends, and whether it renews.
  • We also keep an identifier for each notification Apple sends us about a subscription, for 30 days, so that none is handled twice.

When you set target allocations (optional)

  • The groups you make: each group's name, which you write yourself, the holdings and kinds of holding (for example "stocks") you put in it, identified by the security's identifier and type, and the target percentage you give it.
  • We store this only if you set targets. It is saved as you wrote it, and we don't combine it with anything else.

Every day, while you have a linked account

  • One snapshot of your holdings and their total value, so the app can later show how your portfolio changed over time. It holds the same information as above, nothing more.

What we don't collect

  • Your brokerage username or password, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or anything from your device other than what's listed here.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and the app contains no tracking code.

How we use it

Only to run Orbit: to sign you in, fetch your holdings from Plaid, draw your ring, keep the daily record, keep the targets you set so the app can show how far your portfolio is from them, and keep the service secure (for example, limiting how often someone can try to sign in). We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or anything unrelated to showing you your portfolio.

Who else is involved

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and the App Store subscription, and tells our server when a subscription starts, renews, ends or is refunded. If you turn on crash reporting in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Plaid connects Orbit to your brokerage. When you link an account, you agree to Plaid's End User Privacy Policy: https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy
  • Heroku (Salesforce) hosts our server and database in the United States. Its systems may briefly record technical details of requests, such as IP addresses, as part of running the service.

Apple, Plaid and Heroku handle your data only to provide their services to Orbit, and under their own terms they protect it at least as well as this policy does. We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

How we protect it

  • Everything between the app, our server, Plaid and Apple travels encrypted.
  • Plaid access keys are encrypted again before they're stored, with a key kept separately from the database.
  • Every request is limited to the signed-in user's own data.
  • Our logs never contain access keys, balances, holdings or the names of your groups.
  • On your iPhone, the app keeps your session in the Keychain and can require Face ID to open.

How long we keep it

  • Sessions expire after 30 days, or when you sign out.
  • Your account, linked accounts, holdings and daily snapshots are kept until you disconnect a brokerage or delete your account.
  • Subscription records are kept until you delete your account, including after a subscription ends.
  • Your target allocations are kept until you change or clear them, or delete your account.
  • Backups of the database are kept for up to 14 days, then deleted. So data you delete can stay in a backup for up to 14 more days.

When your subscription ends

If your subscription or free trial ends, or Apple refunds it, we remove your linked brokerage connections 7 days later: Orbit deletes each connection and its access key, stops fetching its holdings and asks Plaid to disconnect it. Your Orbit account and your targets stay, and you can link a brokerage again if you subscribe again.

Disconnecting a brokerage

In the app: Settings → the brokerage → Disconnect. Orbit deletes that connection and its access key, stops fetching its holdings, and asks Plaid to disconnect it. Your brokerage account itself isn't touched.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Orbit then:

  1. deletes everything we store about you (your account, sessions, subscription records, linked accounts, holdings, daily snapshots and target allocations);
  2. asks Plaid to disconnect your linked accounts (your brokerage accounts themselves aren't touched); if Plaid can't be reached, we keep only what's needed to retry until it succeeds;
  3. turns off Sign in with Apple for Orbit.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription: that is managed with Apple (iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

You can also stop Orbit's access from your iPhone's settings (Apple Account → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple), which also deletes your Orbit account.

Your rights

Depending on the state you live in (for example, California), you may have the right to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email aryansumit0102@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using these rights.

Do Not Track

Orbit doesn't track you across apps or websites, so a Do Not Track signal from your browser or device changes nothing.

Where Orbit is offered

Orbit is offered in the United States only, and its servers are in the United States.

Children

Orbit isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
