Orbit Privacy Policy

Effective: November 24, 2026

Orbit shows your brokerage accounts as one ring: what you own, and how it is split. This policy explains what Orbit collects, why, where it goes and how to delete it. Orbit is run by Aryan Gupta ("we"). Questions: aryansumit0102@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Orbit is read-only. It can't trade, move money or change anything in your brokerage accounts.
  • We never see your brokerage password. You sign in to your broker through Plaid, and the password goes to Plaid and your broker, never to us.
  • We keep only what the app needs: your holdings, a daily record of them, and what it takes to keep you signed in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.
  • You can delete your account and everything we store from inside the app, at any time.

What we collect

When you sign in with Apple

  • A user identifier Apple gives us for Orbit. It is unique to Orbit and doesn't reveal your Apple Account.
  • We don't ask Apple for your name or email address, and we don't store them.
  • When you sign in, the app gets a session. We store only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, with when it was created and when it expires (30 days).

When you link a brokerage account (through Plaid)

  • An access key from Plaid that lets Orbit read your holdings. We store it encrypted, and it never leaves our server.
  • Your institution's name (for example, "Robinhood") and Plaid's identifier for the connection.
  • Your investment accounts and holdings as Plaid reports them: account names and the last four digits of the account numbers, the securities you hold, quantities, prices, values and cost basis, and cash balances.

Every day, while you have a linked account

  • One snapshot of your holdings and their total value, so the app can later show how your portfolio changed over time. It holds the same information as above, nothing more.

What we don't collect

  • Your brokerage username or password, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, or anything from your device other than what's listed here.
  • We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and the app contains no tracking code.

How we use it

Only to run Orbit: to sign you in, fetch your holdings from Plaid, draw your ring, keep the daily record, and keep the service secure (for example, limiting how often someone can try to sign in). We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or anything unrelated to showing you your portfolio.

Who else is involved

  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple. If you turn on crash reporting in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Plaid connects Orbit to your brokerage. When you link an account, you agree to Plaid's End User Privacy Policy: https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy
  • Heroku (Salesforce) hosts our server and database in the United States. Its systems may briefly record technical details of requests, such as IP addresses, as part of running the service.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

How we protect it

  • Everything between the app, our server, Plaid and Apple travels encrypted.
  • Plaid access keys are encrypted again before they're stored, with a key kept separately from the database.
  • Every request is limited to the signed-in user's own data.
  • Our logs never contain access keys, balances or holdings.
  • On your iPhone, the app keeps your session in the Keychain and can require Face ID to open.

How long we keep it

  • Sessions expire after 30 days, or when you sign out.
  • Your account, linked accounts, holdings and daily snapshots are kept until you disconnect a brokerage or delete your account.
  • Backups of the database are kept for up to 7 days, then deleted. So data you delete can stay in a backup for up to 7 more days.

Disconnecting a brokerage

In the app: Settings → the brokerage → Disconnect. Orbit deletes that connection and its access key, stops fetching its holdings, and asks Plaid to disconnect it. Your brokerage account itself isn't touched.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Orbit then:

  1. deletes everything we store about you (your account, sessions, linked accounts, holdings and daily snapshots);
  2. asks Plaid to disconnect your linked accounts (your brokerage accounts themselves aren't touched); if Plaid can't be reached, we keep only what's needed to retry until it succeeds;
  3. turns off Sign in with Apple for Orbit.

You can also stop Orbit's access from your iPhone's settings (Apple Account → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple), which also deletes your Orbit account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or get a copy of your data. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email aryansumit0102@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

Children

Orbit isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
